# ---------------------------------------------------------- Wake Word Detection (openWakeWord) ----------------------------------------------------------

1. The Acoustic Gatekeeper (Idle Listening)-

* Continuous Low-Power Scanning: It processes live 16 kHz microphone audio in small 80 ms (1,280-sample) chunks using ~1% CPU overhead.

* VRAM & Hardware Conservation: It keeps your RTX 5060 and faster-whisper in a dormant, low-power state until an intentional wake phrase is spoken.

* Noise and Hallucination Shield: Whisper models can hallucinate or waste compute if fed continuous room noise, keyboard clicks, or background television;
                                the gatekeeper ensures Whisper only receives audio once an explicit command is initiated.

* State Engagement: When a wake phrase ("Atlas", "Buddy", "Pal") crosses the confidence threshold ($\ge 0.6$),
                  it resets its internal recurrent neural memory (oww_model.reset()),
                  flushes stale audio frames from the queue,
                  and switches the system state from IDLE to ACTIVE.



2. The Interrupt Monitor (Wake-Word-Gated Barge-In)-

* Monitoring During Speech: While mouth.py is actively playing synthesized speech through your speakers (mouth.is_speaking == True),
                            openWakeWord continues to evaluate incoming microphone frames in the background.

* Immediate Playback Cutoff: If you speak a wake word while Atlas is talking,
                             openWakeWord spots the trigger and fires an interrupt (mouth.stop()) to kill speaker playback instantly.

* Echo & Feedback Prevention: Unlike generic Voice Activity Detection (VAD)—which would mistake Atlas's own voice from the speakers for your voice
                              and cut him off immediately—openWakeWord requires an exact phonetic pattern match,
                              ensuring speaker bleed does not cause false self-interruptions.

* Zero-Delay Re-engagement: It clears out lingering audio echoes (_flush_queue())
                            and snaps Atlas straight into active listening so you can redirect him without waiting for the previous response to finish


OPENWAKEWORD MODEL ARCHITECTURE BREAKDOWN

1. ACOUSTIC FEATURE EXTRACTION BACKBONE (Shared by all models)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
* melspectrogram.onnx[cite: 1]
  - Acts as the front-end audio feature extractor[cite: 1].
  - Ingests raw 16 kHz, 16-bit PCM audio chunks (80 ms / 1,280 samples) directly from the microphone stream[cite: 1, 2].
  - Converts time-domain audio waveforms into frequency-domain mel-spectrogram representations to emulate human hearing perception[cite: 1].

* embedding_model.onnx[cite: 1]
  - Houses the Google Speech Embedding deep neural network backbone[cite: 1].
  - Accepts the mel-spectrogram output and transforms it into compressed, high-dimensional acoustic feature vectors (embeddings)[cite: 1].
  - Extracts phonetic characteristics from speech, handling over 90% of the system's acoustic computation in a single pass per audio frame[cite: 1].


2. PRE-TRAINED CLASSIFIER HEADS (Phrase Spotters)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
These compact neural network heads evaluate the 76-dimensional embeddings generated by embedding_model.onnx to output a real-time confidence score between 0.0 and 1.0[cite: 1]. Checking multiple heads simultaneously incurs virtually zero additional CPU overhead because the feature extraction stage has already finished[cite: 1].

* hey_jarvis_v0.1.onnx / hey_jarvis.onnx[cite: 1]
  - Trained to classify the activation phrase "Hey Jarvis"[cite: 1].
  - Serves as the default fallback trigger in ears.py when custom models are absent[cite: 1, 2].

* alexa.onnx[cite: 1]
  - Classifies acoustic embeddings matching the keyword "Alexa"[cite: 1].

* hey_mycroft.onnx[cite: 1]
  - Detects the open-source voice platform wake phrase "Hey Mycroft"[cite: 1].

* hey_rhasspy.onnx[cite: 1]
  - Identifies the privacy-focused assistant trigger phrase "Hey Rhasspy"[cite: 1].

* timer.onnx & weather.onnx[cite: 1]
  - Trained to spot functional intent phrases related to setting timers or requesting weather updates directly at the wake stage[cite: 1].


3. CUSTOM WAKE MODELS (Atlas Additions)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
* atlas.onnx, buddy.onnx, pal.onnx[cite: 1]
  - Lightweight custom binary classification layers trained on top of the fixed embedding_model.onnx representations[cite: 1].
  - Evaluate the shared acoustic embeddings in parallel on CPU to awaken the ears.py state machine whenever any of Atlas's specific call names cross the 0.6 probability threshold[cite: 1, 2].
